# MatterChat — Marketing Feature Blurbs

One block per feature: headline, two-sentence value prop, three benefit bullets. Written for law-firm buyers — plain English, no jargon. Status labels reflect staging truth as of 2026-07-01; keep them honest in any external use.

---

## Legal Roles Built In: Partner, Attorney, Paralegal *(live)*

**Your firm's chain of command, ready on day one.**

MatterChat comes with law-firm roles already set up — partners oversee the workspace, attorneys run their own channels, paralegals participate without risk of deleting anything they shouldn't. Assign a role in two clicks and the right guardrails are just there.

- No IT project: pick "partner," "attorney," or "paralegal" from a dropdown and permissions apply instantly.
- Attorneys get real autonomy — they create and run their own matter channels — without holding firm-wide admin keys.
- Every role's powers stay adjustable, so the defaults fit your firm instead of the other way around.

---

## Read Receipts *(landing)*

**Know your message landed — without running a surveillance operation.**

When you tell the team the hearing moved to 9 a.m., a double check mark tells you someone actually saw it. And because delivery confidence and per-person tracking are separate switches, your firm chooses how much visibility is appropriate.

- Single check = sent, double check = read: instant certainty on time-sensitive messages.
- Optional "read by" list shows exactly who has seen a message, and when.
- Privacy by default: both switches ship off, and per-person tracking can stay off firm-wide.

## Boards That Handle Your Whole Caseload *(landing)*

**Every card, every time — no matter how big the board.**

Boards used to silently cut off after 100 cards; now they load everything, and they load it fast. The board paints instantly and streams the rest in behind the scenes, so a 500-card intake pipeline feels as snappy as a 20-card to-do list.

- The 100-card ceiling is gone — your full pipeline is always on screen.
- Big boards open immediately instead of hanging on one giant load.
- Nothing to configure and nothing breaks: existing boards, automations, and integrations just get faster.

## Channel Folders *(live)*

**A sidebar that's organized the way you think.**

Type `/folder Smith v. Jones` in a channel and it files itself under a collapsible folder in your sidebar — one folder per matter, client, or practice group. Folders are personal, so everyone organizes their own view without stepping on anyone else's.

- One command to file a channel; empty command to unfile it. That's the whole feature.
- Collapse a matter you're not working today; its unread count stays visible on the folder.
- Your folders are yours — two attorneys can file the same channel under different matters.

---

## Audit Logging: Privilege Trail, Legal Hold & Retention *(live)*

**Answers for the day someone asks "who authorized that?"**

MatterChat records every change to who-can-do-what — role grants, permission changes, with actor and timestamp — and keeps those records for seven years. Put a matter's rooms under legal hold and automatic retention cleanup won't touch them.

- A complete privilege trail: every role and permission change, attributable and time-stamped.
- Legal hold that actually holds — held rooms are excluded from automatic message pruning.
- Seven-year audit retention out of the box, aligned with legal-industry expectations.

## Org Auto-Provision *(live; end-to-end pending one platform update)*

**Your whole firm is just... there.**

The first time your admin signs in with OmnisAI, MatterChat pulls the firm roster and quietly creates an account for everyone — no invite emails, no spam-folder hunting, no duplicates. Each teammate's first sign-in drops them straight into a ready account with their name on it.

- Onboard 25 people in the time it takes to onboard one.
- Zero invite emails to send, resend, or explain.
- One identity across CasePro and MatterChat — the same OmnisAI login, everywhere.

## Slack & Teams, Inside MatterChat *(live for connect/read/send; real-time sync in progress)*

**Stop alt-tabbing between three chat apps.**

Connect your own Slack workspace or Microsoft Teams account and read and reply to those conversations from a panel inside MatterChat — as yourself, with your own permissions. Unread badges on the workspace rail tell you when something's waiting.

- Co-counsel on Slack, client on Teams, firm on MatterChat — one window.
- Messages send as you, under your own account; the firm never touches your credentials, which are stored encrypted.
- Honest fine print: updates refresh about every 30 seconds today; instant push and full room-to-room bridging are on the roadmap.

## Cross-Firm Secure Messaging *(live; two-firm live demo in progress)*

**Talk to opposing counsel somewhere better than email.**

Dedicated matter rooms connect attorneys at different firms with the guardrails the profession requires: attorney-to-attorney invitations with declared representation, ethical-wall screening, legal hold, and one-click export with a tamper-evident integrity check. Identity is verified server-side on every request — nothing rides on what a browser claims.

- Rule 4.2-aware invitations: representation declared up front, explicit acceptance before anyone joins.
- Hold and export any matter room — the export verifies its own audit chain, ready for production.
- Firm-key trust model: your firm can always access and produce its own records (unlike consumer E2E apps that can lock you out of your own file).

---

## Document Security You Don't Have to Think About *(live)*

**The connection to your files is hardened, quietly.**

MatterChat reaches your LitBox documents through its own server, never directly from the browser, and the credentials it holds are encrypted at rest with industry-standard authenticated encryption. Sessions expire and are enforced — an expired login gets a sign-in prompt, not a file.

- File-access credentials are encrypted at rest and never sent to the browser.
- Expired sessions fail closed: re-authenticate or no access.
- All file traffic flows through one audited, same-origin path.
