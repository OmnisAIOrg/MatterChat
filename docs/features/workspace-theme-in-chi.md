# Switch your workspace theme from Chi

**What it is.** Open Chi (the ensō in the corner), tap the gear, and pick **Workspace
theme**. You get the four appearance modes (Auto, Light, Dark, High contrast) and the six
Paper & Sky tints — Green, Blue, Indigo, Amber, Rose, Graphite — each shown as a swatch of
its real sky gradient.

**Why it matters.** Your whole workspace re-themes instantly — no reload, no digging
through account settings. The pick is saved to your profile, so it follows you from web to
desktop, and it stays in step with the Appearance page (they write the same setting).

**How it works (KB).** The Chi orb's settings panel renders theme choices supplied by the
app and reports your pick back; MatterChat saves it as your `themeAppearence` preference.
Admins don't need to configure anything — the tab appears automatically wherever the
Paper & Sky theme family is available.
